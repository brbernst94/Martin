What's blocking the numbers

In order. Each one gates measurement, not just marketing.

  1. Brand name not chosen

    The critical path item — domain, handles, mailer, landing page and every printed asset wait behind it, with six weeks left. Marty’s call is Standing Invite on standinginvitepress.com. Check the Instagram handle before buying anything. Brian & Patrick

  2. Shipping set to $2.00, still unverified

    Brian corrected it from $0.82 on 9/16. Believable for a large flat, and it barely moves the margin at $32 (85% → 82%). It would move it hard at $15 (72% → 64%). Confirm with a real bundle across a counter once the mailer is designed. Brian

  3. Price not signed off

    Marty’s call is $32/month, $320/year. At $15 the contribution per unit is $10.79 against a category CAC near $72 — a seven-month payback, which means paid acquisition never works and growth is capped at organic forever. Brian

  4. No studio capacity number

    Minutes per bundle is both the growth ceiling and the missing input in gross margin. Patrick’s labour is not in the cost model at all. Patrick

  5. No storefront, so no analytics

    Visits, checkout starts and completion rate don’t exist until something is live to measure. Gifting has to be configured at the same time — it is the 11/1 campaign, not a December feature. Brian

  6. No “how did you hear about us?” at checkout

    Platform attribution is directional at best at our size. One free-text question at checkout is the most reliable acquisition data we will ever get, and it has to exist before the first order. Brian

  7. No tracked links on the Denver ground game

    A coaster drop without a unique short link teaches us nothing. Every physical tactic needs its own URL or QR before a single unit is printed — and coaster art has to reach the printer by 10/1. Marty

  8. No dunning or cancel survey

    Involuntary churn is a quarter to a third of all churn and is pure engineering. Both are launch requirements, not month-two work. Brian
