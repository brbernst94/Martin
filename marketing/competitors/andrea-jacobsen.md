# Monthly Print Club — Andrea Jacobsen

*Checked 2026-09-16 · https://andreajacobsen.com/products/monthly-print-club-subscription*

## Facts

- **Price:** $12.00/month
- **Includes:** one 5×7" postcard art print with an original poem printed on the
  back, plus a personal letter from the artist
- **Shipping:** international, included in price. Dispatches first week of the
  month
- **Commitment:** month-to-month; subscribers can skip individual months or
  cancel anytime from a dashboard
- **Scarcity:** designs are a surprise; past prints only become buyable during a
  "lookback period" at year's end

## What they do well

- **Two art forms in one object.** Image on the front, poem on the back. It's a
  second reason to keep the card and makes a 5×7 postcard feel like more than a
  postcard. Direct analogue for us: a print with the recipe, the history, or the
  argument on the reverse.
- **Skip, not just cancel.** Skipping a month is a far lower-friction exit than
  cancelling and retains the subscription. We should copy this exactly.
- **The year-end lookback.** Turning archive access into a scheduled event
  creates a second revenue moment and makes current subscription feel like
  early access.

## Where they're beatable

- **5×7 postcard is small.** It's a nice object, not a wall piece. Nobody frames
  it. There's no anchor to the bundle.
- **Same predictability problem.** One card, every month, forever.
- **Poem-on-the-back is a narrow taste.** It's a real differentiator but it
  narrows the audience rather than widening it.

## What to steal

1. **Skip-a-month before cancel.** Highest-value mechanic on this page.
2. **Reverse-side content.** Our prints should carry the recipe, the source, the
   story on the back. Free differentiation, and it's the kind of thing people
   photograph.
3. **The year-end archive drop** as a scheduled revenue event.
