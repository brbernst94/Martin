# The Friendly Monthly Print Club — Ravi Zupa

*Checked 2026-09-16 · https://ravizupa.com/products/the-friendly-monthly-print-club*

## Facts

- **Price:** $10.00/month USD
- **Includes:** new 5.5"×8" art print monthly, personalized letter from the
  artist, custom illustrated envelope, chance of randomly receiving an original
- **Paper:** "museum quality, fine art French paper," artist-signed
- **Shipping:** free worldwide, included. Ships last business day of the month
- **Commitment:** none — join, pause, or cancel anytime
- **Cutoff:** join by the 20th for that month's print

## What they do well

- **The letter.** A personalized letter is a retention mechanic disguised as
  packaging. It makes cancelling feel like ending a correspondence.
- **The illustrated envelope.** They understood that the mailer is part of the
  product. We should match or beat this — see `guerrilla.md` #4.
- **The random original.** A lottery element gives every month a reason to open
  the envelope immediately. Cheap, effective, and directly stealable.
- **Pause is prominent.** They surface pausing alongside cancelling.

## Where they're beatable

- **One object, entirely predictable.** Print, letter, envelope, every month.
  Once a subscriber has enough prints there's no second reason to stay.
- **No subject identity.** General illustration. Nobody subscribes because they
  care about the *topic*, only because they like the artist. That's a ceiling.
- **Free worldwide shipping at $10** implies digital printing at volume. Real
  hand-pulled work at that price and that postage doesn't add up.

## What to steal

1. The random-original lottery — costs almost nothing, transforms open rates.
2. The personal letter, but make ours about the *subject*: why this dish, this
   drink, this month.
3. The join-by-the-20th cutoff. Honest, recurring urgency with no discounting.
