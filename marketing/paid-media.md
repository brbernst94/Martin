# Paid media

*Owner: Marty. Status as of 2026-09-16: **OFF. Zero spend. Zero accounts.***

Marty cannot spend money. Everything here is a proposal awaiting Brian's
`APPROVED` marker.

## Why paid is off

1. **No price.** Below roughly $25/month, the math almost certainly never clears
   against a $72 category-average CAC. We can't plan a paid program without
   knowing what we're selling.
2. **No landing page.** Buying traffic to a page that doesn't convert is just
   paying to learn our page doesn't convert. Learn that organically, for free.
3. **No proven creative.** The right ad is an organic post that already worked.
   We don't have organic posts yet.
4. **No volume to read a result.** At small budgets you need weeks to reach
   significance. Spending before there's signal produces confident nonsense.

## The four gates paid has to clear

Paid turns on only when **all four** are true:

- [ ] Price is set and is **$25/month or higher**
- [ ] A landing page converts **cold traffic at ≥3%** to email capture
- [ ] At least **three organic posts have cleared 10k views** — these become the ads
- [ ] Studio capacity has **headroom for 50+ more subscribers/month**

Gate 4 matters as much as the others. Paid that outruns fulfillment is how a
subscription brand kills itself.

## The first test, when gates clear

**Meta (Instagram placements), $30/day for 21 days — $630 total.**

- **Objective:** Email signups to the waitlist, not direct subscription. Lower
  friction, cheaper learning, and the list has value regardless.
- **Creative:** The three best-performing organic process videos, unedited. Do
  not make ads. Run the posts.
- **Audiences:** Three ad sets, equal budget —
  1. Interest: cocktails / mixology / home bar
  2. Interest: printmaking / screenprinting / independent art
  3. Lookalike of the existing waitlist (needs 500+ emails first)
- **The number that decides:** **cost per email under $3.00.** Above $5, kill it
  at day 7 and don't restart.
- **What we learn:** which ICP is cheaper to reach. That answer is worth more
  than the emails.

## Pinterest, second

Cheaper CPMs than Meta, higher purchase intent, and our creative is already
native to it. Test after Meta, same $630 structure, same kill number.

**Do not test Google Search** until there's brand demand or a shop — there's
nothing to capture yet.

## Standing guardrails

- **Never discount to acquire.** Subscribers acquired through heavy discounting
  (free first box, 50% off) churn at 2–3x the rate of full-price buyers. At our
  margins and our churn sensitivity, discount acquisition is negative-value. Use
  a free *extra piece* if we need an incentive — it costs less and attracts
  people who want the art.
- **One variable per test.** No re-platforming mid-flight.
- **Weekly spend cap of $250** without a separate Brian approval.
- **Every campaign gets a kill number written down before it launches.** If the
  kill number isn't in this file, the campaign doesn't run.
- **Attribution will be bad.** Accept it. Use post-purchase "how did you hear
  about us" as the primary source of truth and treat platform-reported
  conversions as directional at best.
