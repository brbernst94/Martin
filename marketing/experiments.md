- **Cost:** ~$300, ~4 Brian-hours building the list, ~2 Patrick-hours signing.
- **Kill date:** 45 days post-mail.
- **Blocked by:** brand name, a print worth sending.
- **Status:** `PROPOSED`

### E-02 · Coaster drop into 15 bars
- **Bet:** 2,000 coasters → 200+ tracked visits → 50+ waitlist signups. Under $8

- **Cost:** ~$400 print, ~6 Brian-hours, ~4 Patrick-hours.
- **Kill date:** 60 days from first drop.
- **Blocked by:** brand name, short-link tracking.
- **Status:** `PROPOSED`

### E-03 · Studio process content, 90 days — one shoot, Instagram + TikTok
