## Subscription box economics

| Metric | Benchmark | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly churn, consumer subscription ecommerce | 5–8% | eightx.co |
| Monthly churn, curated lifestyle/beauty boxes | 7–10% | eightx.co |
| Monthly churn, replenishment/consumables | 4–6% | eightx.co |
| Monthly churn, premium/luxury boxes | 3–5% | eightx.co |
| Monthly churn, best-in-class any category | <3% | eightx.co |
| Average CAC, subscription box | ~$72 | subscriptionboxcalculator.us |
| CAC range | $40–100 | ideafloat.com |
| Minimum acceptable LTV:CAC | 3:1 | subscriptionboxcalculator.us |
| Excellent LTV:CAC | 5:1+ | subscriptionboxcalculator.us |
| Voluntary share of churn | 60–75% | subscriptionboxcalculator.us |
| Churn multiple for discount-acquired subscribers | 2–3x | eightx.co |

**Implication for us:** we sit somewhere between "curated lifestyle box" (7–10%)
and "premium" (3–5%). Hand-made original art at a premium price should behave
like the premium band — but only if the cocktail card solves the wall-space
ceiling. That's a heavier load on the card than it was before 2026-09-16, when
a rotating third object was also carrying it. Target: **under 5% monthly by
month 6.**

**The discount finding is the most actionable line in this file.** Subscribers
acquired with "first box free" or "50% off" churn at 2–3x. At our margins that
is almost certainly negative-value acquisition. See `paid-media.md` guardrails.

## Social engagement benchmarks

| Format | Engagement rate | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram carousel | 0.55% | iqfluence.io |
| Instagram Reels | 0.52% | iqfluence.io |
| Instagram static image | 0.45% | iqfluence.io |

**Implication:** the format differences are noise. What matters is which platform
*compounds*. Pinterest and YouTube are search-driven — a post resurfaces for
months or years. Instagram and TikTok are feed-driven — a post is dead in 48
hours. For evergreen visual product, that's the entire argument for Pinterest.

## Category price points

| Competitor | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ravi Zupa Friendly Monthly Print Club | $10/mo | 5.5×8" signed print, letter, free worldwide shipping |
| Andrea Jacobsen Monthly Print Club | $12/mo | 5×7" postcard print + poem, letter, shipping included |
| Category entry point generally | $10–20/mo | One small print |

**Implication:** the $10–15 band is saturated and assumes digital printing at
volume. We cannot compete there and should not try. Our comparison set is a
bottle of amaro or two cocktails out, not a cheaper print. Note that both
tracked competitors include a letter from the artist — ours is table stakes, and
the photo request inside it is the part that isn't.
