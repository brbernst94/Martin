## One brand, one face

There is no separate "Patrick the artist" account and no separate "the brand"
account. One identity everywhere, with Patrick as the face of it — the Jobs/Apple
model, and the reason that model works is that there was never a second account.
Two accounts at zero followers is dividing nothing in half, and it forces a
"which one does this belong to" decision on every clip, which is what kills
posting habits. Decision and revisit conditions in `company/decisions.md`.

## Shoot once, cut twice

All short-form comes from the same studio session. One filming pass produces:
Instagram Reels (home), TikTok cuts (reach, looser and less finished), and
Pinterest stills. Patrick films during work he's already doing; if it ever
becomes a separate shoot, it dies.

**The content unit is a pull, a failure, or a third-piece reveal.** Not Patrick
explaining anything to camera. His hands are the character — face is optional,
which means none of this is blocked on open question #6.

## Core: Instagram

**Bet:** Studio-process video is the cheapest credible content this business can
make, and both primary segments are dense here. 3 posts/week gets us to 2,000
engaged followers in 90 days.

**What we post:**
- The pull. Squeegee, ink, reveal. 8 seconds. This is the whole format.
- Color separations building up, layer by layer.
- Failures — a ruined proof, bad registration, ink that dried wrong.
- The subject research: the actual cocktail, the actual dish, being made.
- Never: a static photo of a finished print with a price on it.

**Cadence:** 3 Reels/week, 2 stories/day during a print run.
**Owner:** Patrick shoots (phone, no setup), Marty writes and schedules.
**Patrick-hours:** ~1/week if filming happens *during* work he's already doing.
This is the critical constraint — if it becomes a separate shoot, it dies.
**Kill criteria:** Below 500 followers or below 2% engagement at day 90 → cut to
1 post/week and move the hours to Pinterest.

## Core: Pinterest

**Bet:** The most underrated channel available to us. Search-driven, so a pin
posted today still drives traffic in 2028, and "bar cart art," "kitchen wall
art," and "cocktail print" are large evergreen queries. Costs almost no new
creative — it's repurposing.

**What we post:** Every print, shot in a styled context (on a wall above a bar
cart, in a kitchen), 2:3 vertical, keyworded properly. Process shots as idea
pins. 5–10 pins/day is the platform's own sweet spot; ours will be fewer and
that's fine.

**Cadence:** 10 pins/week, batch-scheduled monthly.
**Owner:** Marty entirely, from existing assets.
**Patrick-hours:** ~0 after the initial photo set.
**Kill criteria:** Under 5,000 monthly impressions by day 90 with correct
keywording → deprioritize but never fully kill; it costs nothing to keep.

## Core: Email

**Bet:** The only audience we own. Everything else is rented. The waitlist is
both the launch mechanism and the retention mechanism.

**Pre-launch:** One email every two weeks. Not "coming soon" — actual content.
Behind the plate, the reference photos, the cocktail we're printing and why.
Make the waitlist worth being on.

**Post-launch:** Monthly "here's what's shipping and why" the week the bundle
mails. This email is the single strongest anti-churn tool we have — a subscriber
who opens it remembers why they subscribed.

**Owner:** Marty writes, Brian sends.
**Kill criteria:** None. Email doesn't get killed.

## Core: Physical / guerrilla

See `marketing/guerrilla.md`. Summary: we manufacture small desirable objects,
which means our marketing collateral and our product are the same thing. Almost
nobody else in DTC has that.

## Active: TikTok

*Re-scored 2026-09-16, up from Test. Reason: Brian says Patrick is partial to
short form and to TikTok specifically. The binding constraint on process video
was never the platform's reach — it was whether the artist would reliably film.
That constraint is gone, so the channel is worth real effort.*

**Bet:** Same studio footage as the Reel, cut looser. Nothing there is pretending
to be finished, and short-form printmaking content travels — the reveal is
inherently a payoff shot.
**Reality check that still stands:** commerce intent is low relative to Pinterest
and a post is dead in 48 hours. TikTok buys reach and top-of-funnel; Instagram is
where that reach is asked to convert. Bio points to the waitlist, not a shop.
**Cadence:** 3/week from the same session as Instagram.
**Owner:** Patrick shoots, Marty cuts and posts.
**Patrick-hours:** ~0 marginal. This is the whole reason it's worth doing.
**Kill criteria:** Day 90 (≈ 12/15/26). If no video has cleared 20k views *and*
TikTok has produced under 25 waitlist signups, drop to pure cross-post and put
the editing hours into Pinterest.

## Test: Reddit

**Bet:** r/cocktails, r/printmaking, r/screenprinting, r/bartenders are exactly
our audience and are unreachable any other way. But they will destroy us if we
show up as a brand.
**Rule:** Patrick participates as himself, as a printmaker. He answers questions
about process. He does not link the shop. The payoff is credibility and the
occasional organic "where can I buy that," which converts extraordinarily well.
**Owner:** Patrick, 20 min/week. Marty never posts as Patrick.
**Kill criteria:** Any moderator warning → stop immediately in that sub.

---

## Off: Paid social

Off until we have (a) a price, (b) a converting landing page, (c) organic
creative that already works, and (d) enough volume to read a result. Buying
before those exist is setting money on fire to learn nothing.
Detail and trigger conditions in `marketing/paid-media.md`.

## Off: Search / SEO

No brand demand to capture and no shop to send it to. Becomes interesting the
moment we sell one-off prints, because "screenprint negroni" is a real query
with real buying intent. Revisit then.

## Off: YouTube long-form

A 10-minute studio film would be genuinely great and would cost Patrick a full
day per episode. That's a month's worth of Instagram in one video. Wrong trade
at our size. Revisit if Instagram proves the audience exists.

## Revisit: Markets and craft fairs

Highest-intent environment that exists for this product and the fastest way to
learn what people actually respond to. Also a full weekend of Patrick's time per
event. Do one, post-launch, as research rather than as revenue.
