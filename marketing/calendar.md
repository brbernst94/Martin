## Read this first

The original plan assumed 90 days of audience-building before launch. We have
46, and we don't have a brand name yet. **The 90-day audience phase is gone.**
Pretending otherwise produces a plan that fails quietly. So:

- The waitlist target drops from 500 to **250**. 250 real Denver emails beats a
  padded number.
- Everything not on the critical path to 11/1 is cut or deferred to December.
- The first 100 subscribers come from Denver rooms, by hand. There is no time
  for a channel to compound before launch.

The date itself is right, though. **Open 11/1, deliver 12/1 is the gift window.**
A December-delivered first bundle is a Christmas present that arrives on time.
That's not a consolation — it's the best launch timing this category offers, and
it should be the entire 11/1 campaign.
